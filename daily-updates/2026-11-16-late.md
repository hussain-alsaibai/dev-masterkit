## 2. A guard that covers one path covers none — snapdb 0.18.2

Yesterday's selectivity guard was wired into `execute()` only; `count()`, `sum()`, `avg()`,
`min()`, `max()` kept calling the raw planner. Same predicate, before the fix:

- 1% selectivity: `execute()` 1.1 ms (index) vs `count()` **37 ms** (full scan) — ~34×
- 62% selectivity: aggregation scanned **and** materialised the 162K-element candidate set
  that `explain()` had just reported as rejected (706 ms to build, paid by each of four)

Guard moved into one helper, `_plan_or_scan()`. The durable part is the test: **exactly two
call sites may reach the raw `_plan_query()`**, so a future consumer cannot silently skip
the guard.

Second bug found while writing that test: `min(first, *rest)` lowers to `min(first)` when
`rest` is empty, so `min()`/`max()` raised `TypeError` on a **one-row** result — the common
case for a selective indexed query, i.e. exactly the queries the work had made fast. Newly
fast means newly common; re-test 0 and 1 cardinality when a path gets faster.

Commit `c57aeb3`, CI green. New: `prompts/a-guard-that-covers-one-path-covers-none.md`.

*Known blocker, unchanged:* the repo is at 0.18.2 but PyPI still serves **0.14.0**
(2026-07-03) — 136 days, no `PYPI_TOKEN` in this environment.
