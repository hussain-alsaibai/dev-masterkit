### New Prompt: Silent Failure Detection
- **File:** `prompts/silent-failure-detection.md`
- **Core idea:** Design monitoring systems with liveness probes (active verification), not just state-file checks (passive assumption)
- **Key rules:**
  1. Never trust a "last checked" timestamp as proof of health
  2. Heartbeat-state.json lies if nothing writes to it on each check
  3. External-facing services need active probes, not on-demand health checks
  4. 17-day silent failures are unacceptable for critical infra
- **Last verified:** 2026-07-30
