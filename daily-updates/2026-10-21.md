## Key Events

### The silent no-op

Worth writing down plainly, because the shape generalises. The v0.4.0 and
v0.5.0 sidecars shipped on 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15 as **source files that
were never added to the package manifest**. Every consumer that did
`pip install` got a `tiny_secret_mask` with no path or header redaction in
it. The `except ImportError` fallback returned the input unchanged.

Nothing raised. Nothing logged. `mask_route_path` and `mask_headers` had the
right signatures, returned the right types, and were wired into
`tiny_router_redact_path.py` and `tiny_router_redact_headers.py` exactly as
documented. The code that called them was correct. The guarantee was not.

The 5 failing router tests that surfaced this had been failing since the day
they were written — they were not in any CI run.
