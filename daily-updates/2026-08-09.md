### Key pattern: jsonnet for bounty examples

Bounty specs requiring "no hardcoded prompts" → use jsonnet `std.extVar` to inject parameters. The jsonnet template lives in `jsonnet/main.jsonnet`, secrets in `jsonnet/secrets.jsonnet`, and the TypeScript wrapper calls `evaluateFile()` with `extVars`.
