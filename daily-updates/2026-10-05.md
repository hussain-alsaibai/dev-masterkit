## Key Events

### First Cross-Repo Integration in tiny-* Family

`tiny-log.configure(redact=True)` + `tiny_secret_mask.install_json_default()` is the first **cross-repo** integration. The pattern (lazy sidecar + opt-in flag + idempotent bridge) is likely to spread to `tiny-router` and `tiny-validator` next.

`safe_repr` is the most reusable addition across the family — every other tiny-* lib that touches untrusted data (router, mcp-client, eval) can call it as a pre-serialise sanitiser.

`PATTERN_CATALOG` is now a public spec. External libraries can fork the 18 patterns and adapt the severity tiers to their domain without depending on `tiny_secret_mask` at runtime.
